Visitor Info

Dates, location, transport, registration and on-site services. Search the questions below.

When is it, and what are the opening hours?expand_more
June 25–27, 2026. Thu & Fri 09:30–17:30; Sat 09:30–16:30.
Where is the venue?expand_more
Taipei World Trade Center (TWTC) Hall 1, No. 5, Sec. 5, Xinyi Rd., Xinyi District, Taipei.
How do I get there?expand_more
MRT Red Line to Taipei 101/World Trade Center Station (R03), Exit 1 or 5. By HSR, take it to Taipei Station then transfer to the MRT.
How do I get in / register?expand_more
Free online pre-registration on the official site gives you a QR-code badge; on-site registration is also available at the counter. This is a professional B2B trade fair.
Is there fast-track entry for healthcare professionals?expand_more
Yes. Healthcare professionals (physicians, nurses, therapists, etc.) can submit the designated form for expedited entry with ID verification.
Can children under 12 enter?expand_more
No. During June 25–27, 2026, children aged 12 and under are not admitted to the show floor.
Is there accessible / priority entry?expand_more
Yes. Persons with disabilities, wheelchair users and visitors aged 65+ receive priority entry, each with one companion.
How do group visits work?expand_more
Groups of 10 or more, and academic groups, can arrange a visit by emailing medicaltaiwan@taitra.org.tw.
What about parking?expand_more
On-site parking is about NT$100/hour (08:00–22:00); additional overnight fees apply.
What on-site services are available?expand_more
Accessible restrooms (near Gate 6, 1F), convenience stores and restaurants (2F), luggage storage (B1), mobile charging stations (B1) and a Muslim prayer room (7F).
How do I contact the organizer?expand_more
TAITRA: phone +886-2-2725-5200, email medicaltaiwan@taitra.org.tw, official site www.medicaltaiwan.com.tw.
Are there concurrent shows?expand_more
Yes — Food Taipei runs alongside, including a Healthy Food zone focused on health foods and precision nutrition.
